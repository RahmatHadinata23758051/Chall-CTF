# GraveResonance

Category: Crypto

Difficulty: Easy

This ledger was pulled from a failed cryptographic survey of a burial complex. The notes insist that the three chambers resonate through some ugly shared structure, and the original author was clearly trying very hard to sound smarter than the data deserved.

There are enormous numbers, pompous labels, and a side note about “spectral basis collapse” that may or may not be complete nonsense. If the math looks cursed, good. That probably means the person who wrote it wanted you to panic before you actually checked anything.

Recover the buried message.
